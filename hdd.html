Rules this page applies
  • Unloading time = delivery complete time − vehicle reporting time (overnight handled).
  • Flag: Local over 1 hr or Outstation over 2 hr → Delay, otherwise Not Delay.
  • ETA is cleaned from entries like “7 :00 Am” or “8;30 Pm”; anything unreadable is marked Check ETA.
  • Reporting status: vehicle reported at or before ETA → On Time, after → Delayed, no time yet → Pending.
  • Unloading status: Local ≥ 1 hr or Outstation ≥ 2 hr → Delayed; other routes → Check Route.
  • Dispatch dates without a year (“24-Oct”) are read as .
  • Short shipment: total of Sum of Gross SHORT amount, invoices with a POD shortage noted, split by controllable / non-controllable.
  • POD, OTD and unloading summaries, and outlet-wise OTD, use the same counts as the Summary sheet.
