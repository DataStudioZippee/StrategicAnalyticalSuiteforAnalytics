Key Definitions

👥 Customer Issue: Pending due to customer unavailability, address issue, refusal or rescheduling.
☎ IVR Calling: Done for OUT TAT orders with > 6 days ageing to resolve via RTO or rescheduled delivery.
🚚 Courier Issue: Pending due to courier partner. Bifurcated into IN TAT and OUT TAT.
✉ Data Source: Order status data for both National and Local couriers is taken from Mail only.
🏢 EF Pendency: Pending at our end. Includes RTO confirmations, shipment lost/damage, no way bill etc.
▤ No Way Bill Issue: Order pendency at EF end until way bill is available and updated.
