Columns the file needs

Purchasing Document, Document Date, Supplier/Supplying Plant, Item, Material, Short Text, Order Quantity, Order Unit, Net Price, Net Order Value, Still to be delivered (qty), Purchasing Group and Price unit. Tax Code, Release indicator and Plant are used when present. Dates may be Excel dates, 2026-11-25 or 25.11.2026; SAP trailing minus signs (29,000-) are read as negatives.
