Metrics Logic:
SLA Window = Ideal Delivery End − Marketplace Order Date. 38 minutes or less is a 30 Min order, anything longer is a 45 Min order.
SLA Time = Creation Time + SLA Window. Breach = delivered after the SLA Time.
Deep Pain (DP) = delivered more than 15 minutes after the SLA Time. Breach counts in the KPI report leave DP orders out.
Hour = hour of Creation Time. Order Distance = straight-line km from the Malad store, grouped <3, 3–5 and >5.
Riders = unique Rider User IDs in the hour. Idle Riders = riders who took orders before and after that hour but none in it.
Pincode Report = breached orders by hour and Delivery Pincode. Any pincode not in the usual list is added to West automatically.
Hours shown = for today's file, from 07:00 up to the hour the report is made (the time is in each report heading). For an older file, the full day up to 22:00.
